# Research until the requested conclusion is supported

State the question, intended decision, relevant time period and acceptance criteria. Use GPT 6.1 Sol with low reasoning; do not create research workers unless authorized. Respect source access, privacy and publication scope. Inspect existing evidence before duplicating searches or treating old summaries as current facts.

Search primary sources for technical specifications, product behavior, research claims and institutional requirements. Verify current information when it may have changed. Record source identity, date, exact supporting location and the distinction between direct observations and inference. Do not invent quotations, references or measurements. Keep quoted material within applicable limits.

Compare plausible explanations and alternatives, including evidence that would undermine the preferred conclusion. For software or OSS decisions inspect actual interfaces, license terms, preferred source, tests and integration behavior. A README feature list alone does not prove compatibility. Separate implementation availability from real connection, authentication and operational acceptance.

Produce a substantive answer within the evidence's applicable scope. If a missing observation prevents it, perform another available analysis or verification rather than ending at a list of uncertainties. Preserve the unresolved question when a genuine external constraint remains. Do not convert an unknown into a fabricated affirmative cause.

Deliver the conclusion with supporting sources, relevant tradeoffs and actionable implications. If implementation is requested, carry accepted findings into code, tests and integration instead of stopping at research. Review the whole requested outcome before declaring completion, and retain the evidence needed to reproduce the conclusion.
