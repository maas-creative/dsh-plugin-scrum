# Metadata and identity

Inspect canonical URL, title, structured data, author, publication date and modification date. Retain raw values alongside any normalized dates. Metadata is publisher-provided evidence and may conflict with visible text.

Validate that the canonical URL still identifies the requested item. Resolve relative links against the actual final URL. Do not treat a search snippet, Open Graph preview, or machine-generated summary as the full source.

When dates disagree, cite the visible statement and explain the discrepancy. An article date does not establish a submission deadline, archive capture time, or successful submission.
