# Feeds and pagination

Discover advertised feed links in the publisher's page or documentation. Parse RSS or Atom with an XML parser; disable external entity resolution. Keep entry IDs, canonical links, publication dates and update dates separately.

A feed is often a recent window, not a complete archive. Check pagination or historical access before claiming exhaustive coverage. Deduplicate by stable ID or canonical link while preserving updates. Treat malformed dates as unknown rather than silently assigning today.

Open the canonical entry for details omitted by the feed. For deadline monitoring, record which source was checked and the latest successful retrieval; a failed refresh must not look like no new deadlines.
