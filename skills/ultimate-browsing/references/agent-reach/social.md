# Public social sources

Resolve account identity and stable post links. Separate authored, quoted and reposted content. Keep original dates and inspect attachments needed to substantiate the claim.

Use authorized account access only. Treat third-party mirrors as alternative provenance and flag missing or deleted material. A search result does not prove a post remains visible.

Avoid exporting account cookies or private messages. Sending a reply, following an account or publishing a post is an external action and requires the user's authorization.
