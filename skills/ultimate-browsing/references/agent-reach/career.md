# Opportunities and applications

Open the employer or programme's official posting. Record eligibility, deadline with timezone, required documents, submission destination and contact details. Search listings are discovery aids; verify them against the official notice.

Distinguish an expression of interest, saved draft and confirmed submission. A receipt or visible submission state is needed before marking an application submitted. Preserve conflicting dates and request clarification only when the missing fact affects execution.

Do not apply, upload personal documents or contact an organizer without authorization. Return enough evidence for a deadline record without silently creating or changing the Dashboard ledger.
