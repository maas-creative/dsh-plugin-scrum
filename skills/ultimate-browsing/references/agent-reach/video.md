# Video sources

Identify the publisher, video URL and relevant time range. Use available captions as a starting point, checking important names and numbers against the audio or frames.

Keep quotations tied to timestamps and distinguish captions from a verified transcript. Inspect slides or figures directly when the claim depends on their contents.

If playback or captions are unavailable, state which evidence was actually obtained. A title or thumbnail does not establish the argument of the full recording.
